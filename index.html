Teaching robot hands from human hands

dext builds a forearm band that reads muscle signals and turns them into hand motion and grip-force data, so robots can learn the skills people use every day.

How it works

From the forearm to the robot hand.

Nothing is worn on the hand, so people work exactly as they normally would.

  1. Wear

    A soft band on the forearm, where the muscles that move the fingers sit.

  2. Perform

    Do the task you want a robot to learn, such as picking, assembling or packing, while the band records.

  3. Decode

    Software turns eight channels of muscle signals into finger motion and grip force.

  4. Train

    Robotics teams use the data to teach robot hands that same task.

Built around your robots.

We work directly with robotics companies to develop a data collection solution tailored to their robots, tasks and workflows.
